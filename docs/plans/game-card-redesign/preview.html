Game card · design preview

“Match ticket”

The card becomes a ticket: a tinted stub answers when (the first question every player asks), the body answers what & where, and the footer answers who & can I get in — seats are drawn as seats, not as a progress bar. Entity colour moves from a full-card gradient into the stub only, so a feed of mixed tournaments, trainings and games stays calm. Every fact the current card shows is still here; tags are capped at one row.

Hierarchy

Time is the largest type on the card (22 pt, tabular). Title second. Everything else is 12–13 pt and greyed — it is there when you look, invisible when you scan.

Calm by default

One accent per card (the stub). Tags collapse to a single row with “+N”. Booked / weather / reactions shrink to quiet inline glyphs instead of stacked right-rail panels.

Robust

Title clamps to 2 lines, venue truncates, the stub has a fixed width so long locales never reflow it. Join is a compact pill that keeps the seat count visible; the aria-label carries the full sentence.
