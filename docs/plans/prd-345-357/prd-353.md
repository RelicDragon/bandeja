# PRD 353: PRD: Monthly recap story and share card

## Problem Statement

Stories already auto-generate for game created, results, photos, and bracket champions, and the profile has rich insights (streaks, best partner, nemesis, level history). None of this is packaged into a shareable moment. Players have no monthly reason to open the app or post about it.

## Solution

On the first days of each month, generate a personal **Monthly recap** story for active players: games played, win rate, level change, best partner, longest streak, favourite club. The recap is private by default, viewable by the owner, shareable to followers as a story segment and exportable as an image card using the existing results share card pipeline.

## User Stories

1. As a player who played last month, I want a recap story bubble on Home in the first week of the month.
2. As a player, I want slides for games, wins, level delta, best partner, favourite club, and streak, so that it feels like a highlight reel.
3. As a player, I want to share the recap to my followers' story rail with one tap, so that it is social but opt-in.
4. As a player, I want to export a single summary image to share outside the app.
5. As a player with fewer than 2 games, I want a light "come back" version, not an empty stats reel.
6. As a multisport player, I want one recap with a slide per sport.
7. As a player, I want the recap in my app language and with my time format.
8. As a player, I want a push "Your September recap is ready" that respects reminder prefs.
9. As a developer agent, I want recap generation to be a scheduled job with idempotency per (userId, monthKey).
10. As a player, I want to pick which slides to share, so that I can hide a bad month's level drop.
11. As a player, I want past recaps kept on my profile, so that I can look back.

## UX / UI Design

### Home entry
- A **recap bubble** at the front of `StoriesRail` with a ring in a gradient (sky → violet) instead of the usual story ring, label "Your Sep recap". It gently pulses once on first appearance. Disappears from the rail after viewing but stays in Profile.

### Recap viewer
- Uses the existing story viewer chrome (tap to advance, hold to pause, swipe down to close) with a custom slide set. Each slide is full-bleed with a dark gradient base and one accent color per slide, large numerals with a count-up on slide entry (≤600 ms), and a one-line caption. Slide order:
  1. **Cover**: "September in numbers", the user's avatar, month, sport icons.
  2. **Games**: "14 games" with a 7×4 dot calendar where played days glow.
  3. **Wins**: "9 wins · 64 %" with a radial progress ring drawing in.
  4. **Level**: "3.9 → 4.1" with a mini level-history sparkline; if negative, the copy is neutral ("Level moved to 3.8") and the slide can be excluded from sharing.
  5. **Best partner**: partner avatar and "5 wins together with Ana", chemistry chip if the pairs feature exists.
  6. **Streak**: "Played 4 weeks in a row" with a flame icon and the play-streak chip style.
  7. **Club**: "Home court: Padel Centar · 8 games" with the club avatar.
  8. **Outro**: "See you on court in October" with two actions **Share with followers** and **Save image**.
- Multisport: sport slides are grouped with a small sport tab strip under the progress bars.
- Low-activity variant (0–1 games): three slides: cover, "One game in September", outro "Post a play intent" with a **I want to play** action that opens the intent sheet.

### Sharing
- **Share with followers** opens a compact sheet listing the slides as thumbnails with checkboxes (all on by default except negative level), a preview strip, and **Share** primary. Result: a story with the selected slides in the followers' rails, with the standard 24 h TTL and the usual engagement (likes, comments, replies) enabled.
- **Save image** renders the summary card (games, wins, level, partner, club) in the results share card style with the Bandeja wordmark and the user's name, then uses the native share sheet on mobile or a download on web.

### Profile
- **Statistics → Recaps**: a horizontal row of month cards (month name, games, win %) that open the viewer; kept 12 months.

### Push
- "Your September recap is ready ✨" opens the viewer directly.

### Motion and theme
- Slide transitions: crossfade 200 ms with a 1.02 scale settle; charts draw over 500 ms; all skipped under reduced motion (final state shown immediately). Light and dark share the same dark slide base; Premium members get a gold accent on the cover.

### Accessibility
- Every slide has a text alternative announced on entry; the viewer supports keyboard arrows on web; captions meet contrast on the gradient.

## Implementation Decisions

- New `StorySourceType.MONTHLY_RECAP`; `MonthlyRecap` row (userId, monthKey, payload JSON, viewedAt?, sharedAt?, sharedSlideKeys[], createdAt) unique (userId, monthKey).
- Job: `MonthlyRecapScheduler` daily 04:00 on days 1–3, for users with ≥1 FINAL game in the previous month (or a low-activity variant for users active in the prior 90 days). Payload from `userPerformanceInsights.service.ts`, `LevelChangeEvent` history, `GameOutcome` rows, play-streak fields, and club counts.
- Viewer: FE renders slides from the payload (no server images) inside the existing story viewer via a `recap` segment kind. Sharing creates a `UserStory` with server-rendered slide images through the results artifact renderer (`gameResultsArtifact/`, S3), visible to followers under standard story privacy; unshared recaps never create a story row.
- Summary image: same renderer with a `recap-card` template.
- Push: `NotificationType.MONTHLY_RECAP_READY` → `sendReminders`.
- Retention: `MonthlyRecap` rows kept 12 months (prune in `story.prune.service.ts`).
- API: `GET /users/me/recaps`, `GET /users/me/recaps/:monthKey`, `POST /users/me/recaps/:monthKey/share { slideKeys }`, `POST …/export`.
- i18n namespace `recap`; numbers via `Intl` per locale.

## Testing Decisions

- Payload builder unit tests with fixture months (single sport, multisport, low activity, negative level).
- Scheduler idempotency and day-window tests.
- Share integration: story rows and segment count match selected slides; TTL applied.
- FE: slide rendering per payload shape, share sheet selection, reduced-motion path.
- UI test plan: Home recap bubble, viewer, share, export, Profile recaps row.

## Out of Scope

- Yearly recap (reuse the pipeline later).
- Club or city-wide recaps.
- Video rendering.

## Design language (shared with every PRD in this set)

- **Mobile first.** Capacitor iOS/Android and mobile browsers are the primary target. Every overlay with an input lays out against the visual viewport via the keyboard contract (`Frontend/src/utils/keyboardLayout.ts`, `body.keyboard-visible`, `--overlay-bottom-inset`). Desktop gets the same components in the split layouts already used by Home/Find.
- **Surfaces.** Bottom sheets (vaul) for actions and detail, Radix dialogs only for destructive confirms, full-screen routes for destinations. Sheets open/close with the existing `[data-state='open'|'closed']` keyframes; all motion gated on `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Motion.** Framer Motion 12. Springs for entrance (stiffness 260, damping 24), 180–240 ms fades for state changes, layout animations for list reorders. Never animate more than one hero element at once. Number changes use a count-up tween ≤600 ms.
- **Color.** Tailwind `primary` (sky) for actions, semantic greens/ambers/reds for states, neutral surfaces from `tokens.css`. Premium members keep gold accents where the shell already applies them; new surfaces must read correctly in Light, Dark, Classic, and Premium.
- **Type and density.** One headline per screen, one primary action per sheet, secondary actions as text buttons. Cards keep the `GameCard` rhythm: date tile left, title row, info rows, right rail.
- **Icons.** lucide-react only. No emoji as icons; emoji allowed in copy where playful.
- **Feedback.** `react-hot-toast` for transient confirmations, inline banners for persistent state, skeletons (`GameCardSkeleton` pattern) for loading, `EmptyStateCard` for empties with one clear next action.
- **Accessibility.** 44 px tap targets, 4.5:1 text contrast, roles/labels on every control, arrow-key support on segmented controls, RTL verified for `ar`, all copy in the 11 app locales.
- **Copy.** Plain, second person, verbs first ("Confirm seat", not "Seat confirmation"). No jargon from the schema in user-facing text.
