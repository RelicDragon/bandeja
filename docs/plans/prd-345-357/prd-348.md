# PRD 348: PRD: Cost split tracker (per-head share, paid status, reminders)

## Problem Statement

Game price (`priceType`, `priceCurrency`, `priceTotal`) is display only. After the game, whoever booked the court chases three other people for money in chat. There is no per-head figure, no record of who paid, and no gentle reminder. External payments are out of scope by product constraint, so this must work without moving money.

## Solution

A lightweight ledger on the game: per-head share derived from `priceTotal` and the PLAYING count, a **payer** (defaults to the owner), and a **paid** tick per participant that either the payer or the participant can set. A settle reminder goes to unpaid participants after the game. No money moves in-app; coin `TRANSFER` is offered as one optional way to settle.

## User Stories

1. As an organizer, I want the game to show "Total 40 € · 10 € each" automatically, so that I never compute shares by hand.
2. As an organizer, I want to mark who actually paid the club, so that the ledger knows who is owed.
3. As a participant, I want to tick "I paid" and the organizer to tick "Received", so that both sides agree.
4. As an organizer, I want to override a share for one player (guest, half session), so that edge cases work.
5. As a participant, I want a reminder the day after the game if I am unpaid, so that I do not forget.
6. As an organizer, I want a "Remind unpaid" button, rate limited, so that I can nudge without nagging.
7. As a participant, I want an optional "Pay with coins" that runs the existing P2P transfer to the payer and marks me paid.
8. As a participant, I want my outstanding shares listed in the Wallet, so that I can settle in one place.
9. As a player who left before the game, I want to be excluded from the split automatically.
10. As an organizer, I want the split to recompute when the roster changes before the game and freeze at FINAL.
11. As a substitute, I want to inherit the share of the seat I took.
12. As a developer agent, I want the ledger to be additive and optional; games without a price show nothing.
13. As an organizer, I want to add the payer's bank/IBAN or payment-app handle as free text, so that friends know where to send money outside the app.
14. As a participant, I want to see the per-head price on the card before joining, so that cost is never a surprise.

## UX / UI Design

### Before the game
- **Cards**: when a known total exists, the price row in `GameCardInfoRows` reads "10 € per player" (derived) instead of the raw total, with the total in a tooltip/long-press.
- **Create/Edit**: the existing Price section gains a live preview line "≈ 10 € each for 4 players" that updates as `maxParticipants` changes, and an optional **How to pay you** text field (IBAN, Revolut tag, etc., 120 chars) stored on the game.

### Game details: Cost card
- Placed in the General tab under location. Header "Cost" with the total, currency, and payer avatar "Paid by Marko". Body: a **share row per PLAYING participant**: avatar, name, amount, and a state chip: **Unpaid** (neutral outline), **Marked paid** (amber, participant says paid), **Settled** (green check, payer confirmed). The viewer's own row is pinned to the top with a wide button **I paid** or **Settled ✓**.
- Tapping **I paid** opens a small sheet: "How did you pay?" with **Outside the app** (marks paid) and, when the viewer has enough coins, **Send 10 coins** (runs `SendMoneyToUserModal` prefilled with payer and amount, then marks settled). The payer's payment hint text appears at the top of the sheet in a copyable field.
- Payer/organizer view: each row has a **Received** check toggle; a summary strip "3 of 4 settled · 10 € outstanding" with a **Remind unpaid** button and cooldown text.
- Overrides: organizer long-press or overflow → **Edit share** with a numeric keypad sheet (keyboard-aware) and a "Split remainder evenly" toggle.

### After the game
- At FINAL the card freezes shares and shows a lock icon "Shares fixed at final score" tooltip.
- Reminder push (24 h after FINAL to unpaid): "10 € to Marko for Tuesday's game. Tap to settle." Opens the game with the Cost card scrolled into view and the **I paid** sheet pre-opened.

### Wallet
- The Wallet modal gains an **Owed** section listing outstanding shares as rows (game title, date, amount, payer avatar) each with **Settle** opening the same sheet, and an **Owed to you** section for payers with counts. Empty state: "All settled 🎉" in muted text.

### Motion and feedback
- State chip transitions cross-fade 180 ms; a settled row briefly tints green for 600 ms.
- Toasts: "Marked as paid", "10 coins sent · settled".

### Edge cases
- Price type unknown or 0: card hidden entirely.
- Roster change before FINAL re-splits and shows a quiet "Shares updated" caption for 5 s.
- Substitution moves the share (and paid state) to the substitute.
- Currency is the game currency, no conversion; the coins option converts 1:1 by count only when the platform coin rate setting is present, otherwise coins option is hidden.

### Accessibility
- Chips have text labels, amounts read with currency, toggles are real checkboxes.

## Implementation Decisions

- Schema: `GameCostShare` (gameId, userId, amountCents, currency, markedPaidAt?, confirmedAt?, method MANUAL|COINS, transactionId?, updatedAt) unique (gameId, userId). `Game.costPayerId` nullable, `Game.paymentHint` varchar(120), `Game.costFrozenAt`.
- Shares materialize when `priceType` is a known total and the roster changes while `resultsStatus === NONE`; frozen at FINAL. Owner NON_PLAYING is excluded unless payer. Rounding to cents; remainder to the payer.
- API: `GET /games/:id/cost-shares`, `PUT /games/:id/cost-shares` (owner/admin: payer, overrides, hint), `POST /games/:id/cost-shares/me/paid` `{ method }`, `POST /games/:id/cost-shares/:userId/confirm` (payer/owner/admin), `POST /games/:id/cost-shares/remind`.
- Coins settle: calls the existing `TRANSFER` transaction service with message "Game share · {gameName}"; on success writes `method=COINS`, both timestamps, `transactionId`. Coin amount = `amountCents` converted via platform setting `COINS_PER_CURRENCY_UNIT` (admin Platform Settings); option hidden when unset.
- Reminder: `NotificationType.GAME_COST_REMINDER` → `sendWalletNotifications`; automatic once at FINAL+24 h; manual nudge max once per 24 h per game.
- Wallet: `GET /transactions/owed` returns outstanding shares in both directions.
- Deep link `?section=cost` scrolls to the card; `?settle=1` opens the sheet.
- Feature flag `VITE_COST_SPLIT_ENABLED`.

## Testing Decisions

- Pure share math (rounding, overrides, exclusions, substitution, freeze).
- Service: recompute on join/leave, freeze at FINAL, permission matrix for marks/confirms, coin settle path propagates insufficient-balance error and never double-marks.
- FE: card states for participant vs payer, sheet branches, wallet sections, card per-head price.
- UI test plan rows in Game details and Wallet.

## Out of Scope

- Card payments or any payment provider.
- Splitting across a series (series can aggregate later).
- Club-side invoicing.

## Design language (shared with every PRD in this set)

- **Mobile first.** Capacitor iOS/Android and mobile browsers are the primary target. Every overlay with an input lays out against the visual viewport via the keyboard contract (`Frontend/src/utils/keyboardLayout.ts`, `body.keyboard-visible`, `--overlay-bottom-inset`). Desktop gets the same components in the split layouts already used by Home/Find.
- **Surfaces.** Bottom sheets (vaul) for actions and detail, Radix dialogs only for destructive confirms, full-screen routes for destinations. Sheets open/close with the existing `[data-state='open'|'closed']` keyframes; all motion gated on `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Motion.** Framer Motion 12. Springs for entrance (stiffness 260, damping 24), 180–240 ms fades for state changes, layout animations for list reorders. Never animate more than one hero element at once. Number changes use a count-up tween ≤600 ms.
- **Color.** Tailwind `primary` (sky) for actions, semantic greens/ambers/reds for states, neutral surfaces from `tokens.css`. Premium members keep gold accents where the shell already applies them; new surfaces must read correctly in Light, Dark, Classic, and Premium.
- **Type and density.** One headline per screen, one primary action per sheet, secondary actions as text buttons. Cards keep the `GameCard` rhythm: date tile left, title row, info rows, right rail.
- **Icons.** lucide-react only. No emoji as icons; emoji allowed in copy where playful.
- **Feedback.** `react-hot-toast` for transient confirmations, inline banners for persistent state, skeletons (`GameCardSkeleton` pattern) for loading, `EmptyStateCard` for empties with one clear next action.
- **Accessibility.** 44 px tap targets, 4.5:1 text contrast, roles/labels on every control, arrow-key support on segmented controls, RTL verified for `ar`, all copy in the 11 app locales.
- **Copy.** Plain, second person, verbs first ("Confirm seat", not "Seat confirmation"). No jargon from the schema in user-facing text.
