Find: Looking-to-play Count

Shows "{n} people are looking to play today" in the Find empty state and on the play-intent strip. The number is hidden below three people, per city and sport, and counts only players with a live intent who are not already seated in a game. Read by the app through GET /api/public/platform-flags; clients pick the change up within five minutes.

FIND_LOOKING_COUNT_ENABLED —

Organizer Next Steps

Shows the "Next steps" block under the game header for owners and admins, and folds the attendance strip and open-spot row into it. Off keeps those surfaces exactly as before. Read by the app through GET /api/public/platform-flags; clients pick the change up within five minutes.
