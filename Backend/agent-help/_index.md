# App help topics

Call `get_help` with one of these ids. Roles and permissions are in `roles`; where screens and buttons are is in `ui-map`.

- `roles`: who can see and do what (player, game owner and admins, league season owner and admins, trainer, tournament and league creators, platform admin) and how a user gets each role
- `ui-map`: the app's tabs, screens and main buttons, with their exact names
- `create-game`: create a casual game (or a bar / social event): club, time, players, level, privacy, price
- `create-tournament`: create a tournament: who may, format, teams, rounds, live scoring, results
- `create-league-season`: create a league and a season: who may, groups, schedule, fixtures, standings
- `create-training`: create a training as a trainer, how players book it, and how to become a trainer
- `invite-players`: invite players to a game, the waiting list, accept or decline from the queue, remove players, game admins
- `enter-results`: enter match scores and finish results, who may, and why results can be locked
- `split-costs`: set a game price, split the cost, mark shares paid, pay with coins, remind unpaid players
- `book-court`: find free courts and book one (with or without a game), see and cancel bookings
